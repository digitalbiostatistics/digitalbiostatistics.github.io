Our paper, “Variable Selection for Fixed and Random Effects in Multilevel Functional Mixed-Effects Models,” has been accepted in Statistics in Medicine.
This work introduces the first variable-selection method for multilevel functional models. Congratulations to Rahul Ghosal, Enakshi Saha, and the whole team.
