Why build this research program at MBZUAI in the United Arab Emirates?

Few places today offer the conditions to build an ambitious, interdisciplinary research program from the ground up. The UAE, and MBZUAI, is one of them. Several reasons stand out:

1. Sustained National and Institutional Support

The UAE treats artificial intelligence as a national priority. Its National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031 commits to investment in research, education, and infrastructure, and in 2017 the country became the first in the world to appoint a Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence. MBZUAI is central to this effort, and its vision and mission are aligned with Abu Dhabi's development as a hub for the international AI community. For a research group, this backing is both durable and operational: funding and institutional continuity that outlast any single grant cycle, and approvals fast enough that a proposed line of research can be funded, staffed, and underway without long delay.

This strong institutional support creates a favorable environment for scientists who wish to explore ambitious ideas and develop projects with potentially transformative impact.

2. Faculty and Research Focus

MBZUAI has assembled a faculty of established researchers across computer vision, machine learning, natural language processing, and related fields, with research directed at problems of real-world consequence. The environment rewards substantial, long-horizon work over incremental output, the conditions a program built on a new methodological framework such as digital biostatistics requires.

3. A Crossroads For Global Talent

The UAE sits at the intersection of Europe, Asia, and Africa and draws researchers from China, India, Russia, the Middle East, and beyond. That concentration of scientific traditions in one place makes international collaboration easier to build and sustain, an advantage for a program whose questions cross disciplines and regions.
